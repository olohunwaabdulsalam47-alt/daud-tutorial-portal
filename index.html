About Our Tutorial

Discover who we are and what we stand for.

DAUD TUTORIAL PORTAL is designed to support students in their educational journey through organized learning, academic guidance, discipline, and a commitment to excellence.

Our goal is to create an environment where learners can develop their knowledge, confidence, skills, and character.

Our Mission

To provide quality tutorial education and help learners develop the knowledge and skills they need to succeed.

Our Vision

To become a trusted learning centre known for academic excellence, discipline, and positive student development.

Our Values

Knowledge, discipline, integrity, respect, hard work, responsibility, and excellence.

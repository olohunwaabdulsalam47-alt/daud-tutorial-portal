SUBJECT AREAS

Subjects & Course Descriptions

Subject selection depends on class level and programme availability. Please confirm current offerings with our administration.

Primary School & JSS 1–3

📖 English Language

Reading, comprehension, grammar, vocabulary, spelling, writing and communication skills.

🔢 Mathematics

Numeracy, calculations, mathematical reasoning, problem-solving and confidence with numbers.

🔬 Basic Science

Introductory scientific concepts, nature, the human body, the environment and inquiry.

🛠️ Basic Technology

Basic technological concepts, tools, materials, design awareness and practical problem-solving.

💻 Computer Studies

Computer literacy, device basics, digital skills and responsible use of technology.

🌍 Social Studies

People, communities, culture, environment and how society works.

⚖️ Civic Education

Citizenship, rights, duties, responsibility, values and participation in society.

🌾 Agricultural Science

Plants, animals, agriculture, food production and basic farm practices.

Senior Secondary School (SS 1–3) — Subject Areas

🧬 Biology

Living organisms, life processes, ecosystems and biological principles for secondary learners.

⚗️ Chemistry

Matter, materials, reactions and introductory chemical concepts for secondary learners.

⚙️ Physics

Energy, motion, forces and the physical principles behind everyday phenomena.

📐 Further Mathematics

Advanced mathematical topics and problem-solving practice for learners taking the subject.

📊 Economics

Resources, choices, markets, production, consumption and basic economic ideas.

🛒 Commerce

Trade, business activities, distribution, commercial services and how markets operate.

🧾 Financial Accounting

Basic accounting records, financial transactions, bookkeeping principles and accounts preparation.

📚 Literature in English

Reading and interpreting prose, drama and poetry; literary devices, themes and written analysis.

🏛️ Government

Political systems, institutions, public administration, governance and civic structures.

🕰️ History

Past events, people, societies and historical developments, with context and interpretation.

🕌 Religious Studies

Religious teachings, values, moral development and understanding of selected texts and traditions.

External Examination & Entrance Preparation

🎯 WAEC / NECO Preparation

Guided revision, past-question practice, mock tests, weak-topic review, exam strategies and time management.

📝 JAMB / UTME Preparation

English and candidate-selected subjects, CBT practice, timed mock tests, topic review and examination strategies.

📌 Examination Preparation

Study planning, revision routines, practice questions, confidence building and test-taking skills.

Admission & Registration

Start your learning journey with DAUD TUTORIAL PORTAL.

How to Register

  1. Contact our administration through WhatsApp or email to ask about available programmes.
  2. Provide the learner's basic information and preferred class or examination programme.
  3. Discuss the suitable learning arrangement and current fees with the administration.
  4. Follow the registration instructions provided by the administration.
  5. Wait for confirmation and further joining instructions.

Online student registration is not yet active. The secure Student Portal registration system will be announced when it is ready.

Who Can Apply? — Eligibility

Our tutorial support is intended for learners in the following groups:

  • Primary school pupils
  • Junior Secondary School: JSS 1–3
  • Senior Secondary School: SS 1–3
  • WAEC and NECO candidates
  • JAMB / UTME candidates

Learners should choose the class or examination programme that matches their current learning needs. The administration can provide placement guidance where necessary.

Age & Level Guidance

The ages below are general educational guidance only, not strict admission age limits:

  • Primary School: approximately 6–11 years
  • JSS 1: approximately 12 years
  • JSS 2: approximately 13 years
  • JSS 3: approximately 14 years
  • SS 1: approximately 15 years
  • SS 2: approximately 16 years
  • SS 3: approximately 17 years

WAEC, NECO and JAMB candidates may be of different ages depending on their educational history and examination readiness.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about admission, registration and our tutorial programmes.

Who can join DAUD TUTORIAL PORTAL?

Primary school pupils, JSS 1–3 students, SS 1–3 students, WAEC/NECO candidates and JAMB/UTME candidates can enquire about suitable tutorial support.

How can I register?

Contact our administration through WhatsApp or email for current registration instructions. Online student registration will become available once the secure Student Portal registration system is activated.

Is there an age limit for admission?

The ages shown for Primary, JSS and SS levels are general guidance, not strict age limits. Examination candidates may be of different ages. Contact the administration if you need help selecting the appropriate level.

What programmes are available?

We provide academic support for Primary, JSS and SS learners, subject-based tutorials, and preparation support for WAEC, NECO and JAMB/UTME candidates. Ask the administration about current class availability.

How much are the tuition and registration fees?

Monthly tuition is ₦3,000 for Primary School, ₦4,000 for JSS 1–3, ₦5,000 for SS 1–3, and ₦6,000 for WAEC/NECO or JAMB/UTME preparation. The one-time registration fee is ₦1,000. Monthly tuition covers all subjects in the selected programme. Contact the administration for payment instructions and to ask about possible sibling discounts.

How do I access the Student Portal?

The Student Portal preview is available on this website, but secure student registration and login will be activated and announced separately when ready.

Can the administration help me choose a class?

Yes. Contact the administration with the learner's current class or examination goal to request placement guidance.
